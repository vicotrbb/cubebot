# Code of conduct

Cubebot follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is the short form; the Covenant is the full text.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept critique of the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling and sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sexualized language or attention.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer, [@vicotrbb](https://github.com/vicotrbb). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
