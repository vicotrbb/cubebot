# Cubebot website (site/)

Scope: the whole public site. Home (Persuade), Integrations catalog and detail (Persuade/Read), Resources pages: download, docs/install, docs/architecture, docs/security, docs/features, changelog (Read), About (Persuade). Visitors are self-hosting developers plus curious non-developers. Action: install, then star the repository; secondary: sponsor. Proof: the product inventory (27 connectors, 5 providers, MCP, sandbox runtimes), dated releases, honest status. Constraints: free, open-source, Apache-2.0, self-hosted; never claim unqualified capabilities; keep the current hero untouched; static Astro, no new dependencies; every script function complexity <= 15.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the desk grown into a workshop: every page is built from the same painted blocks, so the catalog, docs and about page feel like rooms of one house, not a template marketing site with a mascot pasted on. It refuses icon-card grids and dark docs chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Warm desk ground, birch shelves, one orange paint, sage for sleeping, paper for infrastructure, raw wood for planned. Rubik heavy and tight. Blocks with darker bottom edges and warm contact shadows; chips as small painted blocks; connectors as small cubes stamped with a monogram; docs pages sit on paper sheets resting on a shelf.

STORY: Home shows what an agent is, what it can reach (the capabilities wall), how it is installed, what runs today. Integrations proves breadth. Resources answer how to run it and why to trust it. About gives the person and the reason. Every page ends with Star and Sponsor.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home keeps the existing hero verbatim. Inner pages open with a short page head: heavy headline left, one cube scene right on a shelf, primary action under the lede.

FORM: Extend the established world (inherit), item 1 of the ordered list; no seed key (surface extension inside an established world, user-specified structure).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the capabilities wall, three vertical columns of painted block tiles drifting at different speeds and directions, pausing on hover/focus, static under reduced motion; plus a live star counter block in the header.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
